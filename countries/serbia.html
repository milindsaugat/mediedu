Step-by-Step Roadmap

MBBS in Serbia Admission Process — How to Apply?

The procedure to get admission in Serbia is standard and streamlined. Here is the step-by-step admission procedure handled end-to-end by MedicEdu Global.

01

Provide Application Documents

Submit your academic documents with us to start your application. We will translate and legalize your documents and then submit your application to the university for academic verification.

02

Entrance Exam

The university will conduct a simple online entrance exam for Biology and Chemistry. MedicEdu Global provides syllabus guidance and sample question papers.

03

Ministry of Education Approval (ENIC/NARIC)

Before proceeding further, we apply with the Serbian Ministry of Education for official educational equivalency (Nostrification / ENIC-NARIC).

04

Visa Application in India

We prepare your visa application form and all supporting apostilled dossier documents. Then, we submit your application to the Embassy of Serbia. You do not need to visit the Embassy yourself.

05

Visa Approval

The Embassy takes about 4–6 weeks to review the visa application and issue the official Serbian student visa sticker on your passport.

06

Travel & Start Studying

You travel with our accompanied student group flight. Upon arrival, our local on-ground team handles university registration, hostel allotment, and Indian mess onboarding.

Requirements & Checklist

Eligibility & Documents Required for MBBS Admission in Serbia

Listed below are the eligibility criteria and documents you need to provide at admission and thereafter for visa application.

50% or More in 12th PCB

Minimum 50% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (Individually) & English in Class XII (40% for Reserved Category).

17+ Years of Age

Candidate must be minimum 17 years old on or before 31st December of the admission year.

Passed NEET-UG

Must have qualified NEET-UG in the current or previous 3 years for the degree to be valid in India.

Important Note on NEET: Studying MBBS in Serbia without passing NEET will result in the degree not getting recognized in India. However, you can work as a doctor in Serbia or other European countries as long as you complete local licensing requirements.
Stage 1

Documents Required for Admission

  • Valid Passport: Clear scanned copy of front and back pages (Min 18 months validity).
  • School Certificates: Class 10th and 12th original marksheets and passing certificates.
  • English Language Proficiency: Proof of schooling conducted in English medium (No IELTS/TOEFL required).
  • Birth Certificate: Official birth certificate issued by the Government authority.
Stage 2

Documents Required for Visa Application

  • University Fee Payment: Payment proof of 1st semester tuition fee transfer.
  • School Certificates with Apostille: Academic marksheets authenticated by MEA.
  • Admission Letter: Official admission letter issued by the Serbian university.
  • Invitation Letter: Official visa invitation approved by Serbian Ministry.
  • Police Clearance Certificate (PCC): Issued by Regional Passport Office.
  • Accommodation & Insurance: Proof of hostel accommodation and travel medical insurance.
  • Proof of Funds: Bank statement to cover tuition and living expenses.
