Study MBBS in Romania: 2026–2027 Fee Structure, Admissions, Top Universities

Romania is a prominent European Union and Schengen Zone member state in Southeastern Europe. Renowned for its rich medical traditions and strict adherence to European Directives (Directive 2005/36/EC), Romania offers Indian students a prestigious 6-year General Medicine (MD / MBBS) degree in English that grants direct practice and residency privileges across all 27 European Union member nations.

Medical Education in Romania

Prestigious EU Medical Training with Automatic European Recognition

Romania has established itself as one of the most sought-after medical destinations for international students from Germany, France, the UK, and India. Medical universities in cities like Iași, Cluj-Napoca, Bucharest, and Timișoara are century-old institutions renowned for cutting-edge medical research, advanced surgical simulation suites, and extensive clinical rotations.

The medical curriculum in Romania is strictly regulated by the Romanian Ministry of National Education and the Romanian Agency for Quality Assurance in Higher Education (ARACIS). It complies 100% with the National Medical Commission (NMC) FMGL Regulations 2021, ensuring full validity for the NExT exam in India as well as licensing exams worldwide like USMLE (USA) and PLAB / UKMLA (UK).

As a Schengen member state, students holding a Romanian student residency permit can travel freely across all Schengen nations, opening exceptional opportunities for clinical observer-ships and summer internships in Western European hospitals.

Step-by-Step Roadmap

MBBS in Romania Admission Process — How to Apply?

The Romanian university admission and Ministry letter of acceptance process is meticulously executed by MedicEdu Global.

01

Document Submission

Submit your 10th and 12th marksheets, passport, and NEET scorecard for initial eligibility and academic file preparation.

02

Academic Translation & Legalization

We translate your educational documents into Romanian, legalize them, and submit your application file directly to the medical university.

03

Ministry Letter of Acceptance (Scrisoare de Acceptare)

The Romanian Ministry of National Education evaluates your academic file and issues the official government Letter of Acceptance.

04

Tuition Fee Transfer & Visa File Preparation

Upon receiving the acceptance letter, transfer the 1st-year tuition fee directly to the university's bank account to generate the payment receipt for visa filing.

05

Romanian Long-Stay Visa (Type D/SD)

We submit your complete visa file (acceptance letter, fee receipt, MEA apostilled marksheets, medical insurance, PCC) to the Embassy of Romania.

06

Departure, Arrival & University Enrollment

Travel with our accompanied group flight. Our local Romanian team handles airport pickup, dormitory accommodation, and in-person university enrollment.

Requirements & Checklist

Eligibility & Documents Required for MBBS Admission in Romania

Listed below are the academic criteria and documentation checklist for Romanian medical university admission and student visa.

50% in 12th PCB

Minimum 50% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (Individually) & English in Class XII (40% for SC/ST/OBC).

17+ Years of Age

Candidate must complete minimum 17 years of age on or before 31st December of the admission year (2026).

NEET-UG Qualified

Must have qualified the NEET-UG examination in the current year (2026) or within the last 3 academic years.

NEET Mandatory for India: NEET qualification is strictly mandatory for Indian citizens desiring to sit for the NExT examination and obtain registration with State Medical Councils in India upon course completion.
Stage 1

Documents for Ministry Acceptance Letter

  • Valid Passport: Clear scanned copy of front and back pages (Min 2 years validity).
  • Class 10th & 12th Marksheets: Certified true copies of academic marks.
  • NEET Scorecard: Official NTA NEET qualification certificate.
  • Birth Certificate: Official birth certificate with English translation.
  • Medical Fitness Certificate: Showing freedom from contagious diseases.
Stage 2

Documents for Romanian Student Visa (Type D)

  • Ministry Letter of Acceptance: Official original government letter.
  • Apostilled Marksheets: 10th and 12th certificates authenticated by MEA India.
  • Tuition Fee Transfer Receipt: Bank swift copy of 1st-year fee payment.
  • Police Clearance Certificate (PCC): Issued by Regional Passport Office.
  • Proof of Accommodation: University dormitory contract or lease agreement.
  • Bank Statement: Parent's bank statement showing proof of financial sustenance.
