Medical Education in Georgia

European Standard Medical Hub with Global Career Pathways

Georgia offers world-class medical education adhering to the European Bologna Process and WFME (World Federation for Medical Education) standards. Medical universities in Tbilisi feature state-of-the-art campus facilities, interactive anatomy simulation suites, and direct affiliation with renowned teaching hospitals such as American Hospital Tbilisi and Tbilisi Central Hospital.

The 5.8-year MD program is conducted entirely in English and strictly follows the National Medical Commission (NMC) FMGL Regulations 2021. Indian students graduate with complete clinical competency, making them fully eligible for the NExT exam in India, USMLE (USA), PLAB / UKMLA (UK), and European medical licensing examinations.

With safe European living standards, direct flights from New Delhi/Mumbai to Tbilisi, affordable monthly accommodation, and dedicated Indian mess facilities, Georgia provides the ideal academic environment for ambitious medical students.

Requirements

Eligibility Criteria & Required Documents

Check the admission criteria and required paperwork for Indian applicants.

50% or More in 12th PCB

Minimum 50% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (Individually) & English in Class XII (40% for Reserved Category).

17+ Years of Age

Candidate must be minimum 17 years old on or before 31st December of the admission year.

Passed NEET-UG

Must have qualified NEET-UG in the current or previous 3 years for the degree to be valid in India.

Stage 1: University Application Documents

  • Original Passport: Minimum validity of 18 months.
  • Class 10th Marksheet & Certificate: Date of birth verification.
  • Class 12th Marksheet: Showing 50%+ PCB score.
  • NEET-UG Score Card: Proving qualified status.
  • Passport-Size Photos: 6-8 color photographs (white background, 35x45mm).
  • Medical Fitness Certificate: With HIV & chest X-ray report.

Stage 2: Visa & EQE Ministry Documents

  • MEA Apostille on Certificates: School marksheets authenticated by MEA India.
  • Official Admission & Recognition Order: Issued by Georgian EQE Ministry.
  • Police Clearance Certificate (PCC): Issued by Regional Passport Office.
  • Proof of Financial Funds: 6-month bank statement supporting tuition & living costs.
  • Travel Health Insurance: Minimum coverage of €30,000.
  • Hostel Booking & Accommodation Proof: Guaranteed university hostel allotment.
