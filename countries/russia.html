Step-by-Step Roadmap

MBBS in Russia Admission Process — How to Apply?

Simple, streamlined admission and visa issuance process handled with full support by MedicEdu Global.

01

Document Submission & Profile Evaluation

Submit your 10th and 12th marksheets, passport, and NEET scorecard for initial university seat allocation and profile review.

02

University Admission Letter

We submit your verified documents to the chosen Russian Federal Medical University to issue your official Admission Letter in 3 to 7 working days.

03

Ministry Invitation Letter (MVD)

The university applies with the Ministry of Internal Affairs (MVD) of the Russian Federation to issue your official electronic Visa Invitation Letter.

04

Visa Application at Russian Embassy

We prepare your complete Russian student visa file, including MEA apostille, HIV test report, and submit your dossier to the Russian Embassy/Consulate in India.

05

Visa Stamping & Ticket Booking

Your Russian Student Visa is stamped on your passport. We coordinate group flight tickets, travel insurance, and pre-departure orientation in Delhi.

06

Arrival, Hostel & University Registration

Our Russian on-ground representatives receive students at Moscow/regional airports, arrange bus transfers to campus, complete hostel room allotment, and register with the university.

Eligibility & Documents Required for MBBS Admission in Russia

Listed below are the qualification benchmarks and document dossier required for Russian medical admissions.

50% in 12th PCB

Minimum 50% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (Individually) in Class XII (40% for SC/ST/OBC category candidates).

17+ Years of Age

Candidate must complete minimum 17 years of age on or before 31st December of the admission year (2026).

NEET-UG Qualified

Must have qualified the NEET-UG examination in the current year (2026) or within the last 3 academic years.

NEET Requirement: NEET-UG qualification is compulsory for all Indian citizens studying MBBS abroad to be eligible for NExT (National Exit Test) and clinical licensing in India.

Documents for University Admission
