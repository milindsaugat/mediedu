Medical Education in Kyrgyzstan

Top Quality Affordable Medical Hub Just 3.5 Hours from India

Kyrgyzstan has emerged as one of the most popular medical study destinations for Indian middle-class families. Renowned public institutions like Kyrgyz State Medical Academy (KSMA) and Osh State University have been training international medical graduates for over 30 years with large patient flows in state hospital complexes.

The General Medicine MD program complies with the National Medical Commission (NMC) FMGL Regulations 2021, providing 54 months of rigorous academic study followed by a mandatory 12-month clinical rotatory internship. All university examinations, theory classes, and clinical demonstrations are conducted in 100% English.

With over 12,000 Indian students currently pursuing medicine in Bishkek, Osh, and Kant, the country provides a safe, welcoming atmosphere with extensive Indian dining networks, celebratory Indian festivals (Diwali, Holi, Eid), and dedicated Indian wardens in student hostels.

Requirements & Checklist

Eligibility & Documents Required for MBBS Admission in Kyrgyzstan

Straightforward eligibility benchmarks and document checklist for Kyrgyz medical academies.

50% in 12th PCB

Minimum 50% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (Individually) in Class XII (40% for SC/ST/OBC category candidates).

17+ Years of Age

Candidate must complete minimum 17 years of age on or before 31st December of the admission year (2026).

NEET-UG Qualified

Must have qualified the NEET-UG examination in the current year (2026) or within the last 3 academic years.

NEET Mandatory for India: NEET qualification is strictly required for Indian citizens to be eligible for NExT (National Exit Test) and practice medicine in India upon course completion.
Stage 1

Documents for University Admission

  • Valid Passport: Scanned copy of first and last pages (Min 18 months validity).
  • Class 10th & 12th Marksheets: Original marksheets and passing certificates.
  • NEET Scorecard: Official NTA NEET qualification scorecard.
  • Passport Photographs: 10 passport-size photos with white background.
  • Birth Certificate: Official birth certificate with English translation.
Stage 2

Documents for Kyrgyz Student E-Visa

  • Ministry Invitation Letter (MFA): Official electronic visa approval.
  • Apostilled Marksheets: 10th & 12th certificates apostilled by MEA India.
  • Medical Fitness & HIV Report: General medical fitness certificate.
  • University Admission Letter: Official provisional acceptance letter.
  • Flight Ticket: Confirmed air ticket from Delhi to Bishkek.
