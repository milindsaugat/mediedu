Medical Education in Uzbekistan

Smart Digital Medical Infrastructure & Rich Silk Road Heritage

Uzbekistan, the historical cradle of Avicenna (Ibn Sina — the Father of Early Modern Medicine), has established ultra-modern smart medical universities. Leading state institutions like Tashkent Medical Academy (TMA), Samarkand State Medical University, and Bukhara State Medical Institute boast newly built digital classrooms, artificial intelligence clinical simulators, and massive teaching hospital networks.

The 5+1 year Medical Doctor program strictly complies with the National Medical Commission (NMC) FMGL Regulations 2021. With 54 months of rigorous theoretical and bedside training followed by a mandatory 12-month clinical rotatory internship, graduates are fully eligible for the NExT exam in India and global licensing exams including USMLE and PLAB.

Indian students in Tashkent, Samarkand, and Bukhara experience a peaceful, culturally hospitable environment with low living costs (~$170/month), secure modern hostels, and dedicated Indian mess facilities serving fresh Indian food.

Eligibility & Documents Required for MBBS Admission in Uzbekistan

Straightforward eligibility benchmarks and document checklist for Uzbek state medical universities.

50% in 12th PCB

Minimum 50% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (Individually) in Class XII (40% for SC/ST/OBC category candidates).

17+ Years of Age

Candidate must complete minimum 17 years of age on or before 31st December of the admission year (2026).

NEET-UG Qualified

Must have qualified the NEET-UG examination in the current year (2026) or within the last 3 academic years.

NEET Mandatory for India: NEET qualification is strictly required for Indian citizens to be eligible for NExT (National Exit Test) and practice medicine in India upon course completion.
Stage 1

Documents for University Admission

  • Valid Passport: Scanned copy of first and last pages (Min 18 months validity).

  • Passport Photographs: 10 passport-size photos with white background.
