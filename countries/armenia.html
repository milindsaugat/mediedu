Medical Education in Armenia

European Standard Medical Training in the Heart of the Caucasus

Armenia is an ancient nation with deep scholarly traditions. Its capital, Yerevan, is home to leading government and international medical universities like Yerevan State Medical University (YSMU) and Mkhitar Gosh University. These universities maintain extensive affiliations with modern multi-specialty clinical hospitals across Yerevan.

The 6-year MD Physician program in Armenia strictly complies with the National Medical Commission (NMC) FMGL Regulations 2021. The program includes 54 months of intensive academic theory and hospital rotations followed by a mandatory 12-month clinical rotatory internship. Degrees are recognized worldwide by WHO, FAIMER, WFME, and the Educational Commission for Foreign Medical Graduates (ECFMG, USA).

Indian students in Armenia benefit from a warm, peaceful society with virtually zero street crime, low living costs (~$200/month), dedicated Indian hostels, and authentic North & South Indian mess facilities.

MBBS in Armenia Admission Process — How to Apply?

The admission process for Armenia is rapid, smooth, and fully managed by MedicEdu Global.

01

Submit Academic Documents

Send your 10th and 12th marksheets, passport copy, and NEET scorecard for initial eligibility verification by our senior medical advisors.

02

University Admission Letter

We submit your academic file to the Armenian medical university to issue your official Admission Letter within 3 to 5 business days.

03

Ministry of Education & Foreign Affairs Approval

Your academic documents are submitted to the Ministry of Education and Science of Armenia for official registration and student dossier approval.

04

Visa Application & Processing

05

Visa Issuance & Flight Booking

Your Armenian Student Visa is issued. We coordinate group flight tickets from Delhi to Yerevan with accompanied student escort.

06

Arrival, Hostel Allotment & Class Start

Our on-ground team receives you at Zvartnots International Airport in Yerevan, arranges hostel check-in, Indian mess registration, and campus orientation.

Eligibility & Documents Required for MBBS Admission in Armenia

Simple admission benchmarks and document checklist for Armenian medical universities.

50% in 12th PCB

Minimum 50% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (Individually) & English in Class XII (40% for SC/ST/OBC).

17+ Years of Age

Candidate must be minimum 17 years old on or before 31st December of the admission year (2026).

NEET-UG Qualified

Must have qualified the NEET-UG examination in the current year (2026) or within the last 3 academic years.

NEET Mandatory for India: NEET-UG qualification is compulsory for all Indian students to be eligible for the NExT licensing exam and practice medicine in India.
