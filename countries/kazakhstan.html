MBBS in Kazakhstan Admission Process — How to Apply?

The admission and visa process for Kazakhstan is fast, transparent, and managed completely by MedicEdu Global.

01

Submit Academic Documents

Send your 10th and 12th marksheets, passport copy, and NEET scorecard for initial eligibility verification by our senior medical advisors.

02

University Admission Letter

We submit your academic file to the chosen Kazakh National Medical University to issue your official Admission Letter in 3 to 5 business days.

03

Ministry Invitation Letter (MIGRATION)

The university applies with the Migration Police and Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Kazakhstan to issue your official electronic Visa Invitation Letter.

04

We process your official Kazakh Student E-Visa through the Ministry portal or submit the dossier to the Embassy of Kazakhstan in Delhi.

05

Group Departure from Delhi

We coordinate group flight bookings from Delhi to Almaty with accompanied student escort and complete pre-departure briefing.

06

Arrival, Hostel Allotment & Class Start

Our on-ground team receives you at Almaty International Airport, arranges bus transfer to campus, hostel check-in, and Indian mess onboarding.

Requirements & Checklist

Eligibility & Documents Required for MBBS Admission in Kazakhstan

Straightforward eligibility benchmarks and document checklist for Kazakh national universities.

50% in 12th PCB

Minimum 50% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (Individually) in Class XII (40% for SC/ST/OBC category candidates).

17+ Years of Age

Candidate must complete minimum 17 years of age on or before 31st December of the admission year (2026).

NEET-UG Qualified

Must have qualified the NEET-UG examination in the current year (2026) or within the last 3 academic years.

NEET Mandatory for India: NEET qualification is strictly required for Indian citizens to be eligible for NExT (National Exit Test) and practice medicine in India upon course completion.
Stage 1

Documents for University Admission

  • Valid Passport: Scanned copy of first and last pages (Min 18 months validity).
  • Class 10th & 12th Marksheets: Original marksheets and passing certificates.
  • NEET Scorecard: Official NTA NEET qualification scorecard.
  • Passport Photographs: 10 passport-size photos with white background.
  • Birth Certificate: Official birth certificate with English translation.
Stage 2

Documents for Kazakh Student Visa

  • Ministry Invitation Letter: Official electronic visa invitation.
  • Apostilled Marksheets: 10th & 12th certificates apostilled by MEA India.
  • Medical Fitness & HIV Report: General medical fitness certificate.
  • University Admission Letter: Official provisional acceptance letter.
  • Flight Ticket: Confirmed air ticket from Delhi to Almaty.
