Medical Education in Bosnia

World-Class European Clinical Hub in the Balkans

Bosnia and Herzegovina shares borders with Croatia, Serbia, and Montenegro. As an official European Union candidate country, Bosnia follows the Bologna Process higher education framework with European Credit Transfer and Accumulation System (ECTS). Its medical faculties boast centuries-old teaching hospital affiliations, state-of-the-art diagnostic labs, and modern clinical simulators.

The 6-year Medical Doctor (MD / MBBS) degree awarded by Bosnian state universities fulfills every requirement laid down by the National Medical Commission (NMC) under the Foreign Medical Graduate Licentiate (FMGL) Regulations 2021. Graduates are granted full licensing eligibility and can practice across Europe, return to India for the NExT exam, or pursue USMLE (USA) and PLAB (UK).

With low living costs (~€250–€300/month), hospitable local population, safe environment, and Indian food arrangements, Bosnia stands out as the #1 value-for-money European medical study destination.

Step-by-Step Roadmap

MBBS in Bosnia Admission Process — How to Apply?

The admission process for Bosnia is structured and handled end-to-end with 100% documentation support by MedicEdu Global.

01

Document Submission

Submit your 10th and 12th marksheets, passport, and NEET scorecard for initial eligibility verification by our senior advisors.

02

University Admission Letter

We submit your translated and notarized academic dossier to the medical faculty in Bosnia to secure your official Provisional Admission Letter.

03

Ministry Equivalency & Nostrification

We process your educational certificates through the Ministry of Education in Bosnia for official nostrification and academic equivalency recognition.

04

Visa Dossier & Embassy Submission

We prepare your complete embassy visa file, including MEA apostilled certificates, travel health insurance, hostel guarantee, and proof of funds.

05

Visa Issuance

The Embassy issues your Long-Term Student Visa (Type D) sticker on your passport, typically completed in 3 to 5 weeks.

06

Group Departure & Onboarding

Fly with our accompanied student batch. Our local team in Bosnia welcomes you at Sarajevo airport, completes hostel check-in, and finalizes university registration.

Requirements & Checklist

Eligibility & Documents Required for MBBS Admission in Bosnia

Simple eligibility guidelines and structured document checklist for admission and student visa processing.

50% in 12th PCB

Minimum 50% aggregate marks in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (Individually) & English in Class XII (40% for SC/ST/OBC).

17+ Years of Age

Student must be at least 17 years old on or before 31st December of the admission year (2026).

NEET-UG Qualified

Must have qualified the NEET-UG examination in the current year (2026) or within the last 3 academic years.

NEET Mandatory for Indian Practice: NEET-UG qualification is strictly required for Indian citizens to be eligible for the NExT examination and medical registration in India after graduation.
Stage 1

Documents for University Admission

  • Valid Passport: Clear scan of first and last pages (Min 18 months validity).
  • Class 10th & 12th Marksheets: Official certificates and mark statements.
  • NEET Scorecard: Official NTA NEET qualification scorecard.
  • Passport Photos: Recent white background passport-size photographs.
  • Birth Certificate: Government-issued birth certificate.
Stage 2

Documents for Embassy Visa Application

  • Apostilled Marksheets: 10th and 12th certificates authenticated by MEA India.
  • University Admission Letter: Official acceptance letter from Bosnian university.
  • Proof of Tuition Fee Transfer: Bank receipt of 1st semester fee payment.
  • Police Clearance Certificate (PCC): Issued by Regional Passport Office (RPO).
  • Medical Fitness & Insurance: Medical test reports and international travel insurance.
  • Financial Sponsorship: Parent's bank statement showing adequate funds.
