Regulatory Compliance

Strict Compliance with NMC FMGL Regulations 2021

Every university we partner with is thoroughly audited against the National Medical Commission (NMC) Foreign Medical Graduate Licentiate guidelines to ensure your medical degree is 100% valid for practicing in India.

  • Course Duration Compliance: Minimum 54 months of classroom theoretical education and clinical rotations.
  • Mandatory 12-Month Internship: Completed in the same parent medical university hospital before graduating.
  • 100% English Medium: Complete curriculum, laboratory training, and examinations conducted in English.
  • Equal Registration Rights: The degree grants direct eligibility to practice medicine as a physician in the awarding country.
