The 6-Stage Roadmap

Your Complete Path to Becoming a Global Doctor

Our proven step-by-step roadmap ensures zero delays and complete peace of mind for parents and students.

01

Free 1:1 Profile Counselling

Our senior advisors evaluate your Class 12 PCB marks, NEET scorecard, and total budget to shortlist the best medical universities in Bosnia, Serbia, Romania, Russia, and Central Asia.

02

Eligibility & Document Review

Verification of academic marksheets, NEET qualification, and passport in strict compliance with the National Medical Commission (NMC) FMGL Regulations 2021.

03

Official Admission Letter

Submission of your application to the university's admissions committee and issuance of your official Admission Confirmation Letter within 5–7 working days.

04

MEA Apostille & Translation

Complete document attestation from the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), notary verification, and translation into the host country's official language.

05

Student Visa Stamping

Preparation of the official visa dossier, appointment booking at the respective Embassy/Consulate, interview preparation, and visa sticker stamping.

06

Flight, Airport Pickup & Mess

Accompanied group flights with student managers, warm reception at the destination airport, university registration, hostel allotment, and Indian mess onboarding.

Required Documents

Mandatory Document Checklist for MBBS Abroad

Keep these original documents ready for a smooth and fast admission process.

Academic Records

  • 10th Standard Marksheet & Certificate
  • 12th Standard Marksheet (PCB Min 50%)
  • School Transfer / Migration Certificate

Identity & NEET

  • Valid Indian Passport (Min 18m validity)
  • NEET-UG Official Scorecard (Qualified)
  • 10–12 Passport Size Photographs (White BG)

Medical & Legal

  • Medical Fitness Certificate
  • HIV / Blood Test Report
  • Bank Statement for Visa Dossier
Important Notice: MedicEdu Global provides complete assistance with document apostille, translation, and notarization. You do not need to visit MEA offices yourself.
