Why MBBS Abroad is the Best Option for Indian Students

You have a lot to gain by choosing to study MBBS abroad. Not only is it vastly more affordable than private medical universities in India, but there is also world-class clinical infrastructure and global licensing opportunities.

Affordable MBBS Education

Overseas medical universities provide high-quality education for tuition fees starting at ₹15 to ₹35 Lakhs total, way lesser than private colleges in India.

Zero Donation / Capitation

Direct merit-based admissions on Class 12 marks and NEET qualification. No unfair donation or high capitation fees required.

100% English Medium

All theoretical lectures, lab sessions, clinical discussions, and examinations are conducted in English throughout the 54-month curriculum.

High Clinical Hospital Exposure

Partner universities are affiliated with massive government teaching hospitals, giving students hands-on patient diagnostic experience.

Worldwide Degree Validity

Degrees are recognized by WHO, NMC, ECFMG, and FAIMER, enabling graduates to practice in India (NExT), Europe, USA (USMLE), and UK (PLAB).

Indian Hostels & Authentic Mess

On-campus university hostels with 24/7 security, high-speed Wi-Fi, and authentic Indian vegetarian & non-vegetarian meals cooked by Indian chefs.
